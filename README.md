

# Mood Journal

"Mood Journal" will help you keep track of your moods and thoughts.

### How to Use
1. **Open `moodjournal.py`:** Rate your day and enter your thoughts.
2. **Storage:** Your thoughts will be stored in an image according to your rating (1=sad, 2=neutral , 3=happy, 4=very happy), along with the time, in the `mood` folder.
3. **Review Entries:** In the `mood` folder, you can find all your data entries and look back at how you were feeling on past days.
